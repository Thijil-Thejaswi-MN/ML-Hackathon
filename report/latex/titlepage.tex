\section*{Executive summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Executive summary}
\markboth{Executive summary}{}
Each trip provides a 1\,Hz GPS track and the trip's own directed road network; the task is to assign one road arc to
every GPS observation and return a connected route. We project each trip to local metres, derive one reference arc per
observation from the given route with a monotone dynamic programme, generate up to 30 candidate arcs per observation
(adaptive 50--200\,m radius), describe every observation--candidate pair with 37 features, and train a binary
classifier that scores how likely each candidate is the true arc. An HMM decoded with the Viterbi algorithm then picks
the most plausible \emph{connected} sequence of arcs, and a shortest-path step stitches it into a continuous route. The
full pipeline is served by a FastAPI service on laptop~1 and driven from a Streamlit evaluator UI on laptop~2.

Five models were trained under one protocol (5-fold cross-validation grouped by trip); LightGBM was best on every
selection criterion and was tuned with 40 trials of Bayesian optimisation (Optuna TPE).

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Candidates:} the true arc is among the candidates for 99.90\,\% of student\_test observations.
\item \textbf{Classifier:} tuned LightGBM reaches CV macro F1 0.9453\,$\pm$\,0.0151 on train and 0.9441 on
student\_test, with log loss 0.0423.
\item \textbf{HMM:} improves Top-1 on 7 of 10 trips and raises the arc-set F1 of the route against the reference from
0.891 to 0.965; decoding costs 253\,ms per 1000 observations.
\item \textbf{Deployment:} the live two-laptop system reproduces the offline numbers exactly (e.g.\ trip 00000007: 99.26\,\%
HMM, 96.53\,\% classifier).
\end{itemize}
